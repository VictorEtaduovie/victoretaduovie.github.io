About — Short & Long

Short: I build production-grade web backends and cross-platform mobile apps focused on performance, security and measurable business outcomes.

Long (preview): I architect and ship scalable Laravel backends (APIs, queues, payment flows) and robust Flutter apps with offline-first sync. I focus on maintainable code, CI, and measurable impact — from database tuning and caching to polished UX.

My typical responsibilities include system design (DB schema, indexing), API design (REST, auth tokens), payment workflows (Stripe webhooks & idempotency), background processing (queues & workers), and mobile sync strategies (sqflite, delta updates). I also do frontend polish using Bootstrap, AJAX and modern JS patterns.

I love writing clear APIs, improving performance, and shipping features that move business metrics. By grace — I aim to do exceptional, measurable work.
